The Burra School

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

Burra's early public school is placed in the wider history of education for children in the mining townships. The booklet explains the reliance on small private and church schools, the fees that kept many poorer children out of classrooms and the education reforms of the 1870s. It follows the opening of the Burra Model School in 1878 and the continuing difficulties caused by poverty, illness and irregular attendance. Details about compulsory schooling and the introduction of free education show how quickly conditions changed, but also why schooling remained difficult for many working families.
