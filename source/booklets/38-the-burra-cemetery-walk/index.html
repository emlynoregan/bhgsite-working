The Burra Cemetery Walk

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

A self-guided tour of Burra Cemetery compiled by Meredith Satchell and Eric Fuss in 2014. The route draws attention to particular graves, inscriptions and memorials, using the lives of people buried there to explore the early townships and surrounding district. It also explains the controversial clearing and levelling of part of the old cemetery in the 1960s, and why some surviving headstones no longer stand at their original burial positions. The booklet combines a map, photographs, personal histories and cemetery interpretation. It is intended to accompany an actual visit, but remains useful as a source of researched local stories.
