Booklet collection · workshop draft · 2017 list

Burra History Group booklets

The Burra History Group's 2017 list identifies 39 locally produced booklets. Original Publisher files and new PDF and HTML exports have been recovered for 38 of them; the separate 28-page The Burra Institute is still missing.

These entries are draft descriptions for review. The 38 recovered booklets can be read online or downloaded as PDFs or original Publisher files. The material is published on this Working site for review; editorial descriptions and responsive adaptations may still be corrected.

  1. 01

    Burra Creek Heritage Walk

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A self-guided walk along Burra Creek through the historic township of Kooringa.

  2. 02

    Burra Fires

    A5 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · edition/page-count check

    A collection of accounts of serious fires in Burra, including the destruction of the Miners' Arms Hotel in December 1878.

  3. 03

    Burra Floods

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    Burra Creek's floods are recalled through accounts of sudden water rises, damaged homes and extraordinary escapes.

  4. 04

    Burra Hotels

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    An introduction to Burra's early hotels as places of refreshment, lodging, business and sometimes disorder in the mining townships.

  5. 05

    Burra’s Monster Mine

    A4 · 60 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A substantial illustrated history of the Burra Burra Mine, the enterprise that helped transform the young colony of South Australia after copper was found in 1845.

  6. 06

    Burra Story

    A5 · 100 catalogue pages · Recovered · edition/page-count check

    An extensive visitor-oriented introduction to Burra and the townships that grew around its famous copper mine.

  7. 07

    Burra Walk of Fame

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The people remembered in Burra's Walk of Fame are presented through short biographies rather than a simple list of names.

  8. 08

    Cornish Beam Engines

    A5 · 4 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A compact explanation of the large steam-powered beam engines that made deep mining at Burra possible.

  9. 09

    Cornish Connection

    A5 · 28 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A history of Burra's links with Cornwall and of the migrants who crossed the world to work in South Australia's copper industry.

  10. 10

    Dare’s Hill Circuit

    A5 · 16 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A touring guide to Dare's Hill and the country north and east of Burra.

  11. 11

    Johnny Green

    A5 · 4 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The four-page booklet tells the story of Johnny Green, the miners' mascot that once stood high above Burra's engine houses.

  12. 12

    Kooringa Methodist Church Windows

    A5 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A guide to the history and decorative windows of Kooringa's Methodist church.

  13. 13

    Market Square Walk

    A5 · 24 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A guided walk through Burra's Market Square and nearby commercial streets, beginning at the Visitors' Information Centre.

  14. 14

    Miners’ Dugouts

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · edition/page-count check

    The miners' dugouts beside Burra Creek were among the most unusual features of early Kooringa.

  15. 15

    Paxton Square

    A5 · 4 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The row of small cottages known as Paxton Square tells part of Burra's story of accommodation, work and community life.

  16. 16

    Burra Railway Station

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The railway's arrival in Burra on 29 August 1870 was a major event, greeted by a large crowd and ceremonial trains from Adelaide.

  17. 17

    Redruth Gaol

    A5 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    Redruth Gaol's later uses make this more than a history of a prison building.

  18. 18

    Snow in Burra

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    Snow is an occasional and memorable event in a district better known for hot summers, dry weather and drought.

  19. 19

    The Bon Accord Mine

    A5 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The Bon Accord Mine was developed as a speculative venture on land north of the more successful Burra Burra Mine.

  20. 20

    Thames Street Cottages

    A5 · 4 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    Among the earliest permanent dwellings built for mine employees were the double cottages in Thames Street, Kooringa.

  21. 21

    The Burra Burra Mine

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A concise illustrated history of the Burra Burra Mine, from the discovery of copper in 1845 to the growth of the great South Australian Mining Association workings.

  22. 22

    The Burra Institute

    A5 · 28 catalogue pages · Source not yet located

    The Burra History Group's 2017 catalogue lists a 28-page booklet titled The Burra Institute.

  23. 23

    The Burra Institute and Town Hall

    A5 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The building on Market Street that became Burra's Town Hall began life as the Miners' and Mechanics' Institute, opened in 1857.

  24. 24

    The Burra Mines

    A5 · 4 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A short illustrated booklet separately listed as The Burra Mines in the 2017 Burra History Group catalogue.

  25. 25

    The Burra School

    A5 · 4 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    Burra's early public school is placed in the wider history of education for children in the mining townships.

  26. 26

    The Burra Smelter

    A5 · 4 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A compact history of the smelting works associated with the Burra copper mine.

  27. 27

    The Churches of Burra

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    Burra's mining townships supported a varied religious life, reflected in the churches and chapels built by different communities.

  28. 28

    The Early Townships

    A5 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    An introduction to the distinct settlements that eventually became Burra.

  29. 29

    The Unicorn Brewery

    A5 · 4 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The Unicorn Brewery is included in the Burra History Group's 2017 booklet catalogue as a four-page publication.

  30. 30

    The Redruth Methodist Church Windows

    A5 · 12 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    This guide places the memorial windows of Redruth's Methodist church within the history of its chapel and congregation.

  31. 31

    Windows of St Mary’s Church

    A5 · 28 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A guide to the stained-glass windows of St Mary's Church in Burra, arranged to follow a numbered plan of the building.

  32. 32

    The Mongolata Goldfields

    A5 · 36 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The story of the Mongolata goldfield brings a later chapter of mining into Burra's history.

  33. 33

    The Telegraph Office

    A5 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    Burra's former Telegraph Office, now used as an art-gallery building, offers a history of communication and public service.

  34. 34

    Burra Creek Guided Walk

    A5 · 28 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A detailed guided walk along Burra Creek and through parts of historic Kooringa, compiled by Julian Ratcliffe for the Burra History Group.

  35. 35

    The S.T. Gill Collection

    A4 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    This booklet is the Burra History Group's short collection associated with the colonial artist S.

  36. 36

    Smelting Burra’s Ore

    A5 · 24 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A longer account of the technology, economics and people behind smelting copper ore from the Burra Burra Mine.

  37. 37

    The Burra Jinker

    A5 · 8 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    The Burra Jinker was an extraordinary heavy-haulage vehicle used to move massive mining machinery inland from the coast.

  38. 38

    The Burra Cemetery Walk

    A4 · 26 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A self-guided tour of Burra Cemetery compiled by Meredith Satchell and Eric Fuss in 2014.

  39. 39

    The Kooringa Walk

    A4 · 20 catalogue pages · Recovered · draft description

    A walk through Kooringa and along Burra Creek linking natural surroundings with the township's mining history.
