The Burra Burra Mine

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

A concise illustrated history of the Burra Burra Mine, from the discovery of copper in 1845 to the growth of the great South Australian Mining Association workings. It contrasts the fortunes of the successful northern lode with the Princess Royal venture and describes the miners recruited from Cornwall and Wales. The booklet explains the underground shafts, copper minerals, horse-powered lifting and the eventual need for steam pumps as water entered the deeper levels. Photographs and descriptions of engine houses give visitors a framework for interpreting the mine ruins. This is the twelve-page title, distinct from the shorter booklet called The Burra Mines.
