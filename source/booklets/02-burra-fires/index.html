Burra Fires

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

A collection of accounts of serious fires in Burra, including the destruction of the Miners' Arms Hotel in December 1878. Rather than merely listing dates, the booklet draws on testimony, inquiries and contemporary reports to reconstruct how fires developed and how residents and firefighters responded. Its hotel account describes the landlord, lodgers, cellars and early attempts to save the building, giving a sense of the dangers in tightly built nineteenth-century commercial premises. The recovered Publisher edition has sixteen pages, although the older printed catalogue lists eight; it therefore needs an edition check before being described as a definitive reprint.
