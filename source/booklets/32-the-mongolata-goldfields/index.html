The Mongolata Goldfields

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

The story of the Mongolata goldfield brings a later chapter of mining into Burra's history. The booklet records discoveries of gold-bearing rock, the renewed excitement following Henry Byles's 1930 find and the creation of mining operations and a government battery during the Depression years. Contemporary reports describe hopeful prospectors, mining camps, ore crushing and the difficulties of finance and work in a period of widespread unemployment. The narrative follows production, investment and the changing fortunes of those involved. Illustrations and references to surviving remains help locate a venture that was quite different from Burra's nineteenth-century copper mines.
