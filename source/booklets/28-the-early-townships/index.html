The Early Townships

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

An introduction to the distinct settlements that eventually became Burra. The booklet begins with Kooringa, planned as the South Australian Mining Association's private town, and explains the company's cottages, miners' dugouts and the growth of a settlement along Burra Creek. It then follows the establishment and roles of Redruth and other nearby townships, including the different patterns of land ownership and government services. The gold-rush departures of the early 1850s and later changes in population show how uneven the town's growth could be. A useful orientation for anyone puzzled by Burra's several historic township names.
