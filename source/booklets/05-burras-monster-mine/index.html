Burra’s Monster Mine

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

A substantial illustrated history of the Burra Burra Mine, the enterprise that helped transform the young colony of South Australia after copper was found in 1845. The booklet follows the discoveries attributed to William Streair and Thomas Pickett, the competing interests behind the Special Survey and the development of the South Australian Mining Association's rich ore body. It also pays attention to the people and techniques of the mine: Cornish miners, Welsh smelters, machinery, transport and difficult living conditions. The narrative continues into the later preservation and reuse of the mine landscape, including the story of Johnny Green, its famous miners' mascot.
