Market Square Walk

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

A guided walk through Burra's Market Square and nearby commercial streets, beginning at the Visitors' Information Centre. The booklet leads readers past some of the town's earliest buildings and later Victorian streetscape, explaining how Kooringa developed as a mining and business centre from 1846. Its stops include former offices, the historic town pump, the Telegraph Office and other commercial and civic buildings. It also points out remnants of mining equipment that have become public heritage features. Rather than isolating monuments, the walk invites visitors to see how shops, banks, transport and public services were clustered around the square.
