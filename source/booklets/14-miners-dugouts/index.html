Miners’ Dugouts

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

The miners' dugouts beside Burra Creek were among the most unusual features of early Kooringa. This illustrated booklet explains why workers and their families excavated homes into the creek's clay banks, how those dwellings appeared in William Cawthorne's 1850 drawing, and the risks from repeated flooding. It follows the survival and later restoration of a few dugouts near Blyth Street, including work undertaken in the early 1970s to preserve a visible reminder of the mining years. The selected recovered edition has eight pages, but both an earlier four-page file and a catalogue listing of twelve pages exist, so this is an edition-specific account.
