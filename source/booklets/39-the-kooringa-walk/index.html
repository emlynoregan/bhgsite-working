The Kooringa Walk

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

A walk through Kooringa and along Burra Creek linking natural surroundings with the township's mining history. The route starts near Market Square and passes buildings and objects associated with early banks, transport, miners' cottages and the development of the settlement. Among its examples are the National Bank and the Burra Jinker, whose machinery-hauling history is briefly explained. The guide connects individual stops with broader themes of life beside the creek and the growth of Kooringa. It is a separate route booklet from Burra Creek Heritage Walk and Burra Creek Guided Walk, and its instructions should be checked against present-day conditions before being followed.
