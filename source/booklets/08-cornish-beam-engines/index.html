Cornish Beam Engines

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

A compact explanation of the large steam-powered beam engines that made deep mining at Burra possible. The booklet introduces the operating principles of atmospheric and Cornish pumping engines, then describes machinery associated with Roach's, Schneider's and Morphett's engine houses. Details of cylinder sizes, boilers, beams and pumping capacity give a sense of the extraordinary engineering involved in keeping the mine dry. It also includes a recollection of how imposing the machinery seemed to a child who visited an engine house. Readers visiting the mine ruins can use this account to imagine the lost machinery within the surviving buildings.
