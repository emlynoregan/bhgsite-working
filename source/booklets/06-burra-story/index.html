Burra Story

The draft description is based on the recovered booklet and has not yet been editorially reviewed.

An extensive visitor-oriented introduction to Burra and the townships that grew around its famous copper mine. The booklet explicitly sets out to tell more than the history of mining, using a series of shorter thematic accounts and places that can be visited on foot or by car. It discusses the development of Kooringa and other townships, civic institutions, work, transport and the changes that followed the mine's closure. Historical photographs and maps help connect the stories to streets and buildings. The recovered edition runs to 108 pages, whereas the 2017 catalogue records 100, so the exact edition deserves checking.
