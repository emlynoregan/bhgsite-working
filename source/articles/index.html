As of 21 September 2026 they are not on Preview either. Creek Street, Trees, and Johnny Green came off Preview onto this site (BHG-024). Preview /features/ URLs now point here.

The working pattern is the one we used for Johnny Green:

  1. The article — one Feature-length narrative.
  2. The research dump — source pages the article can cite: transcriptions, publication hunts, illustration notes, identification caveats. Not a second Feature.

Creek Street and Trees are articles without dumps (yet). Johnny Green, Elizabeth Ward, and the chicken song have both.

Do not splinter one writer across several Features unless a later piece has its own subject. A quotation that already lives in another article (Ward in Johnny Green) can stay there and be pointed at from the new article.
