The Burra Boys

A proposed Feature about Rob Zacher and Don Griffen, their historical songs, and the making and 1983 launch of The Burra Boys. Same split as Ward and Johnny Green: one article, and a research dump the article can cite. Not on Preview yet. Real is not in play.

  • Singing Burra Back to Itself — the readable article: performances and competitions, the record and its songs, Paxton Square launch, folk-label context, preservation and rights.
  • Research dump — artefact inventory, track-level evidence, contemporary chronology, external sources, uncertainties. Plus a source-photograph inventory without the images themselves.

Before Preview

  • Listen to the physical record and verify titles, names, lyrics and side order against the disc labels.
  • Make archival scans of the sleeve, labels and complete lyric sheets.
  • Confirm illustration permissions. Description does not clear complete lyrics, audio, sleeve photography or artwork for republication.
