The Burra Boys — source photograph inventory

These are byte-for-byte copies of the seven photographed JPEG uploads supplied in the 1 October 2026 conversation. No cropping, rotation, recompression, sharpening or OCR has been applied to these images. The images capture the original artefacts but are not archival scans of the physical items.

See the background dossier and working lyric transcriptions (planning repository only; not published here).

Rights and public access

Kept for internal BHG/BDA research. This package does not grant permission to republish the album, sleeve designs, lyric sheets, photographs, or full lyric texts publicly. Confirm rights and provenance before publishing on an open website. In particular, a private research repository and a public GitHub repository have different exposure: check the destination repository's visibility before importing these files.
