CLEANING FOOTPATHS.

To the Editor “The Burra Record.”

Sir,—Seeing this matter has lately been under discussion, and it has been stated that ratepayers could be compelled to clean corporation footpaths near their own property, I would like to ask: “Do the Corporation expect the ratepayers of Hill and Ware Streets, also of a portion of Commercial Street, West Ward, who are mostly widows and single women, to turn out as a pick and shovel brigade doing work for which we pay heavy rates?” My late father was a member of the Burra Town Council for many years; I also have been a ratepayer for over 35 years, and this is the first time I have heard of ratepayers being asked to do Corporation employees’ work. I may say the streets mentioned are much neglected, and are a disgrace to the town. If we take on the job to what rate of wages are we entitled? Would not the Corporation employees have the right to protest against the statement? They also are ratepayers. Perhaps someone will enlighten me.

Yours sincerely,

ELIZABETH A. WARD.
Hill Street, Kooringa,
January 3rd, 1924.
