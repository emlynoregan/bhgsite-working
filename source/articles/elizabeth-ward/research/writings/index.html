This directory gathers every Elizabeth A. Ward text so far recovered. Each document contains the text, its newspaper provenance, a link to the facsimile or hosted clipping, and a statement of transcription status. It is a research corpus, not yet a critical edition.

The distinction between text located and publication known only from a notice is essential. Eric Fuss's Burra In The News summaries are invaluable finding aids, but they are not substitutes for Ward's words; a summary is never presented here as her text.

Editorial method

  • Newspaper titles and dates identify the primary sources.
  • Links lead to Trove facsimiles or to clippings preserved in Eric Fuss's Burra In The News compilation.
  • Dialect is not modernised.
  • Machine transcription errors are corrected only when the reading is secure. Doubtful passages remain flagged for comparison with the facsimile.
  • Familiar or borrowed verse is not assumed to be Ward's composition merely because she quoted it.
