Mother's Memories.

A tiny form, a mother's arms enfolding.
Restling so sweetly in its downy nest,
From harm secure, that loved face be
holding,

So peacefully at rest.
A busy schoolboy, noisy and important ;
Yet mindful of his parents and his
home.
Culling the fruits from many a tree of
knowledge
Prepared for days to come.
With passing years he changes into man
hood,
His heart beats high with manhood's
hope and joy.
His future bright, his dreams are near
accomplished,
But still, he's mother's boy.
But hark! a call comes over many waters
Insistent, stirring, sounding from afar.
He hears, he heeds, nor for a moment
falters,
'Tis Britain's call to war.
A long farewell ; sped on by mother's
blessing
And many prayers, he sallies forth to
fight,
Through blood and fire with courage
never ceasing,
His battle-cry ' For Liberty and
Right.'
o o o o o
'Tis eventide; a woman softly weeping,
Reads o'er the message fraught with
pain,
'Fallen in action' is the fateful tidings,
Her pride, her boy, is with the slain.
She lifts her eyes "Oh God, he's in Thy
keeping,
Thou hast but taken what I freely gave,
His life was given for other lives a ran
som,
Himself he would not save"
Sweet memories come to calm her
troubled breast.
Her boy is still her own, he's but 'At
Rest.'
— E. A. Ward, Burra, S.A.
