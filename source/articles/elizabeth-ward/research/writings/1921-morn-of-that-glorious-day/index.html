Text

The Morn of that Glorious Day,
April 25th, 1915.
Let it never be forgotten,
Tell the glorious tale,
Our gallant boys are landing there
In the waning moonlight pale.
Straight on they go to face a foreign foe,
Out into water deep.
Forward they march amidst the maxim's
fire
And charge up Gaba's steep.
Mid the blinding hail of missiles,
On they ever go,
With steady step and gleaming steel,
Rout the flying, conquered foe
"Come." they shout, "Come on Aus
tralians, Come,"
"On," "Forward," " Make way."
And we know our gallant boys fought
hell
On that glorious day.
Throughout the land of Australia,
In the homes of her noble sons,
Every heart shall swell as the tale they
tell
As they carved her name and the vic
tory won.
Neath the hail of murderous shrapnel
shell,
Up the hill they charge. Nobly they
fell
On the awful hillside of Gallipoli,
On the morn of that glorious day.
April 13th, '21. Elizabeth A. Ward,
Hill St. Kooringa.
