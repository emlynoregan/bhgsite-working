Anzac Day
APRIL 25th, 1942.
AUSTRALIA KNOWS. -
'Twas in Trafalgar Bay,
On that Great Naval Day
The famous signal ran .
'England expects that every man
This day will do his duty.'
Now War is over all
On land, on sea in the air
Bugles ring out the call
For R.A.A.F.
The A.I.F. and R.A.N.
Australia knows her fighting men
This day will do their duty
On noble ship, on soaring 'plane
In battle fierce beneath the rain
Of bursting shells they stand
In manliness and beauty
Where' ere they be in heavier
blows
Her gallant sons Australia knows
Each day will do their duty.
Elizabeth A. Ward,
Hill St., Burra
