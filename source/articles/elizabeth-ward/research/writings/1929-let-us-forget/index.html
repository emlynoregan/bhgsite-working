LET US FORGET

K. reply to ' 'Bewildered' ? re 'The
Guns.'

'Let us forget.' 'Tis long since war
was over,
One cannot ever linger o'er the past,
Memories aye, e'en the keenest do

not last forever
We can be thankful that they do not
last
'Let us forget.' On with the dance
and. pleasure, - ? - .* ? '
Time flees so quickly when the light
and gay,
Fling memories to the windg and trip
the measure
'Let us forget' and cast dull care
away.
'Can we forget?' Shame! shame
upon a people,
Who would blot out a record of our
fame,
Who would consign to darkness and!
oblivion
The men who made Australia's glori
ous name,
'Can we forget?' They were our boys
who suffered
And bore the heat and burden of the
day,
Hunger and thirst, the war and toil
of battle
With Mother's name upon their lips
they passed away.
'Can we forget' that we're Austra
lian women?
Sheltered and safe — because our
brothers died;
Suffered and fought to gain our bles
sed freedom?
'Can we forget them?' Once they
were our pride!
ELIZABETH A. WARD,
Hill Street, Kooringa.
