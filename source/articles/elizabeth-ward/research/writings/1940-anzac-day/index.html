Anzac Day

ANZAC DAY— APRIL 25th, 1915.

Momentous morn, which ushers in
a Nation's birth,
And shows to wondering King
dom's of the Earth

How strong a tie exists between
Australia and the Mother
land.
Proving the valor of her sons .
When landing on the shores of
stern Gallipoli.
They scaled her cliffs and rushed
into the fight
Winning from friend and foe en
during praise.
Their gallant deeds and long en
durance prove
How men can fight and die
For those thev love

To many valiant deeds and lives
laid down this day
The grateful tribute of our Anzac
Day.
ANZAC DAY— APRIL 25th, 1940.
Again "The call to War" another
foe has risen
More subtle than the one their
fathers fought before
From whose assault kingdoms all"'
torn and crushed and bleed
ing lie
For help rings out a cry.
Like as an echo of brave Henry's
cry
"Once more into the breach dear
friends."
Britain and France respond, and
to their aid come the Domin-

ions.
Again old Egypt wakes to hear
The tramp of marching feet
And soldiers tread the hills of
Palestine
A language strange is heard, the
wondering people cry,
"Australians! Australians!"
"Australians in the streets of old
Jerusalem."
May God defend their cause and
help these sons to fight
As bravely as their fathers fought
in days of yore
Giving them victory
Proving to all the Nations of the
World
God, and not Hitler reigns for ever
more.
Elizabeth A. Ward.
Buna, South Australia, 1940.
