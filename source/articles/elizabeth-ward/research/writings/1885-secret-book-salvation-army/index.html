THE SO-CALLED SECRET BOOK OF THE
SALVATION ARMY.

TO THE EDITOE.

Dear sir,—In your issue of yesterday there appears a
letter headed, " Mr. H. Warren in explanation." There is
one passage which I cannot let pass uncontradicted. He
says "The secret book, the one the Army will not sell, nor
let the public see, if they can hinder it." It is not true
that the book is not sold or seen by the public, as I have
seen them publicly exposed for sale, and have been asked
to purchase them. Also, if any one will take the trouble
to look at the War Cry, they can read for themselres the
contents of the secret book published under the heading,
"The Doctrines and Discipline of the Salvation Army."
By giving this letter publicity, you will oblige,
Yours, etc.,

E. A. WARD,

Burra, February 7, 18813.
Salvation Soldier.
