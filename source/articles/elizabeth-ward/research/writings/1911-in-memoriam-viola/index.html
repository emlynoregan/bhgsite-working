" Viola," died September 19 th, 1911
Thou hast laid down the viol,
Singer of sad, sweet song ;
For thee is o'er the weary toil
Of suffering oft, and long.
Lover of tree and bird,
And every living thing ;
By these thy muss hath oft baen stirred
Touch'd thy " Viola's" string.
Memories of thee are sweet
As "Almond Blossoms" bloom,
They breathe with fragrance all replete
Their perfume o'er thy tomb.
Thou hast not lived in vain,
Life doth not ooant in years ;
And thou wert glad, despite of pain—
Thy courage conquered fears.
Too soon thou passed away,
Thy life great promise gave ;
And sorrowing, 1 ia memory lay
This tribute on tby grave.

E. A. WARD, Burra.

("Almond Blossom" was the last poem
written by Viola.)
