Text

"Our Beloved Dead."

When the shades of eve are falling,
And your hearts are sad with fears,
Do you hear their voices calling
"Oh Beloved dry your tears."

We are still ''On Active Service,"
Messages of hope we bring,
We have passed to higher duty,
And we serve a Greater King.
We have learned a noble lesson,
Loss of life to us means gain,
Life Eternal is our guerdon,
E'en though won through strife and
pain.
And we still are near you ; nearer
Than perchance we e'er have been,
For we see with vision clearer
Now there is no veil between.
Oh take comfort, mother, sisters,
Wife still loved as much as yore,
Parents, brothers, all our loved ones,
Meet us when your labours o'er.
Burra, South Australia, E. A. WARD.
October 15th, 1917.
