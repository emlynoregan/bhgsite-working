Text

ONE FALLEN

(J. Camp.)

One fallen in the fight,
One hero passed away ;
Life given for the right,
In battles' awful fray -

One Burra boy.
The first to head the roll,
Of noble lives laid down ;
The first to yield his soul,
The first to win renown —
One Burra boy.
Aye ! drop for him this tear,
Of sympathy and pride ;
Naught may we lay upon his bier,
But this— he hath not died,
Our Burra boy.
If in remembering hearts,
To live in memory sweet,
Is not to die. Tho, thus we part,
We hope again to greet,
Our Burra boy.
E. A. Ward,
Burra, S.A., May 31, 1915.
