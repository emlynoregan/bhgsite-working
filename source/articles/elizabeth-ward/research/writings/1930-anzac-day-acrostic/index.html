ANZAC DAY.
April 25th, 1915-1930.
An April morn; a given task,
Nation.-, look on and wonder more,
Zenith is reached; Why? doth one ask
A nation's born upon that shore.
Called by the voice of Motherland
Dee^s there were wrought which thro'
all time
Australia's fame enduringly shall
s'and,
Youths' sacrifice ennoble evermore.
Elizabeth A. Ward,
Hill Street, Kooringa.
Woods' Great Peppermint Cure
For Coughs and Colds, never fails.

Fully qualified chemists watch
every process in the manufacture of
Lactogen. The whole factory is as
spotless as a new pin.
SEE us before accepting travellers'
statements that we cannot do your
printing as well aid as cheaply as
their firms. We can, and in addition,
you keep your money in your own
town. 'Record' Office
