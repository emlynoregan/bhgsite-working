The booklet quotes:

> In 1936, Elizabeth Ward wrote: I remember him in all his glory standing erect upon the big engine-house, pick and gad in hand, a representative of ‘that theere Cornish Miner’. … He was always referred to as ‘that there Man up to mine’ …

That is the later-reception evidence the researcher wanted: Ward was not entirely forgotten; an anecdote from 1936 was reused, while her identity and larger body of work dropped out of the heritage literature.

The same booklet also repeats the unsourced Green-family-of-Narrogin claim (Henry / “Johnny” Green, Cornish stonemason). That sentence is copied into 9-Burra's Monster Mine.pub as well. No dedicated Narrogin source file turned up on Meredith’s drive.

Do not use the E Drive file named Collections\Publications\Booklets\21-Johnny Green.pdf. Despite the name, that PDF is Burra’s Hotels.

2. The Burra School, 2007

We have it. This is the 2017-list A5 four-pager.

FileNotes
7-Burra School-2007.pubMeredith Burra Booklets-2010/7-Burra School.pub
7-Burra School-2007.pdfAlready on the E Drive booklets pile; copied here
7-Burra School-2007.txtExtracted text

Compiled by Julian Ratcliffe, Burra History Group, 2007. The only reference given is Ian Auhl, The Burra Model School 1878–1978.

It covers private schools before the Model School opened in January 1878, fees, “pauper” children, epidemics, “mushroom schools” after 1878, St Joseph’s, the 1913 high school, and the 1978 community school. It does not name Elizabeth Ward, School Days 1870–1874, Mrs F. J. White, Hetty Symonds, Grammer’s Cat, or “That there Man”.

So it is not the recycled newspaper memoir the researcher hoped for. It is still the BHG booklet they asked to see.

Auhl’s centenary book itself was not found as a PDF or Word file on the drive. NLA: 2803399.

3. School daze!, 1995

We do not have the booklet. What looks like a digital copy is only the cover and Robyn Page’s foreword.

NLA: Burra Jubilee 150 Committee 1995, School daze!: an account of the schools throughout the Burra and district 1845–1995, Burra, bib 905362. No personal author in that record. Preview Resources now uses this citation (BHG-005).

Physical copies are listed for the Ian Auhl History Room, Burra School Community Library, 7 Bridge Terrace, Burra (National Trust Burra resources page: many of these titles are out of print or never formally published, but held there).

What is on the drive

Meredith Documents\Publications\School Daze.pub (9.2 MB, last saved 15 November 2017) is not a typeset book. Publisher embeds three Robert Reed photocopies plus later school photographs (Hallett sports c.1931, Copperhouse, Mt Bryan). No School Daze 04 or later page exists under that name on the working copy, C Drive zip, or extracted E/F Drive trees.

None of that names Ward. Whether the later pages quote School Days 1870–1874 is still unknown.
