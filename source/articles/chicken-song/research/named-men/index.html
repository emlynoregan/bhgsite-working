The identifications live in A Voice from Hill Street. This page is only a pointer so the chicken-song dump does not duplicate the Ward footnotes.

Ward’s mine boys name three adults:

  • Cappen Jinimy — possibly Captain James Roach (surface captain; 1862 birth notice locates him at the Burra Mine; 1889 memorial). “Jimmy” is a familiar form of James; no source yet supplies “Jinimy.”
  • Harry Cock — highly likely a real mine worker. 1869 Kooringa parody names Harry Cock; 1873 assessment places Henry Cock in a four-room house on company property at the Mine. Occupation and authority still unknown.
  • Cappen Dick — possibly Richard Goldsworthy (“Captain Dick” in an early settler diary; long-serving underground captain in mining histories). Graded, not solved.

These names are what make the Burra text a localisation of a travelling rhyme, not a second biography of Elizabeth Ward. Cite the Ward article for the records; do not upgrade the identifications here.
