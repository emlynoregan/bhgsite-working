Folktrax title index

The archive files “When I was a chicken as big as a hen” under Over the Garden Wall. The Oz/A–Z index prints Tricia’s enquiry in full and then lists recordings.index

Tricia (Australia) asked about a song her grandmother sang. Grandmother born 1889 in Australia; grandfather from Northern Ireland; grandmother from England. Version:

When I was a chicken as big as a hen
Me mother hit me so I hit her again
Me father came out and gave me a clout
So I upped with the hammer and flattened him out
Me cruel mother killed me
Me poor father ate me
Me little sister sucked me bones and laid them on a log

“It was sung to what sounds like a little Irish tune.” Email as printed by Folktrax: irishj49@yahoo.com.au. We have not contacted her.

Do not confuse this children’s rhyme with the dance tune also indexed as “Over the Garden Wall” (Rose Murphy / Topic / Ossian). Different item.

FTX-201 programme

One Two Three A-Loopa: playground singing games, skipping, and ball-bouncing. Wyn Humphries, headmaster at Sidbury, talks about how games are passed on.

  • Track 13: OVER THE GARDEN WALL (talk before) — 1′17″ — in the 1951 block.
  • Track 47: OVER THE GARDEN WALL (Ball) — 0′54″ — Kentish Town.

These are the two public catalogue handles for “actual audio of related traditional performances” named in the article. We still need to hear them.

Bill Smith, 1980

Musical Traditions transcription of the chicken stanza inside “Over the Garden Wall” (Roud 19232):

Over the garden wall
You let the baby fall
Me father came in and he ordered me out
And I asked him what it was all about
He said “You let the baby fall
Over the garden wall.”
Now when I was a chicken as big as a hen
Me mother hit me and I hit her again
Me father came in and he ordered me out
And I asked him what it was all about
He said “You let the baby fall
Over the garden wall.”

A later verse is scatological (WC / “pissed on me”). It is printed in the Musical Traditions notes. Do not put it on the Feature. The notes then said Roud had fifteen instances, mostly Scotland and Ireland.

Andy Rodoni, 1993

Andy Rodoni interviewed by John Meredith with Rob Willis, 10 December 1993, John Meredith folklore collection, National Library of Australia, catalogue 2893518. Access open for research. Contents include item 5: When I was a chicken as big as a hen. Australian sung version; not yet heard for this draft. A good next listen after Folktrax, because it is local to this country and already catalogued.
