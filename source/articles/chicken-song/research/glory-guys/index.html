Working notes for the YouTube copy used in When I was a chicken. Not a film essay.

The copy to cite

  • Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8_rHWckxRjM
  • Start time for the song: 1:11:11
  • Embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/8_rHWckxRjM?start=4271 (1 × 3600 + 11 × 60 + 11 = 4271 seconds)
  • Deep link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8_rHWckxRjM&t=4271s

James Caan’s Dugan sings “When I was a chicken as big as a hen…” and further lines of hitting father and mother back. Another character asks, in substance, “That is an Irish tune, is it not?” Dugan agrees.

Do not quote more of the scene than we have actually checked against this copy. Do not treat the Irish-tune line as folklore scholarship; it is dialogue in a Western.

Two clocks

Standard Blu-ray subtitles (as indexed on SubtitleCat) place a substantial stretch of the scene near 1:14:38. That timestamp does not match this YouTube file. Preserve 1:11:11 for the online copy. If a different upload is used later, re-time it; do not assume 4271 seconds travels.

What this source is good for

  • A heard, citable sung version of the chicken family, with a stable public URL.
  • A second, independent association of the song with an Irish tune (the other is Tricia’s grandmother, born Australia 1889 — see Folktrax).

What it is not

  • Evidence that Burra sang this melody.
  • Evidence that Elizabeth Ward sang this melody.
  • Evidence that the rhyme originates in Ireland.
