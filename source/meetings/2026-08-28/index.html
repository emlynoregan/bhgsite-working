Purpose

Record a live pass through Preview so Emlyn can transcribe it and turn comments into site work. Barbara asked to leave maps until last (interactive map is the hardest). Bob’s notes almost all lead back to maps anyway; they still started with townships, then About/contact/help, Features, Resources, then cutover.

Decisions

  • Cut over by end of 2026 so they can start 2027 on the new site. Bob agreed. Nobody wants to wait for a perfect Resources dump.
  • Parity bar: don’t block cutover on problems that already exist on burrasa.net. Don’t go backwards. Do fix organisational copy, contact, and a decent home page.
  • Navigation on Preview is better than the old site (more cohesive, cross-referral). Categories are a good start and a bit uneven (sparse People vs a universe in Resources).
  • Contact: one funnel. All queries through the contact page to the group email. Stub must actually work before cutover.
  • Shop / for-sale leftovers: strip remaining “for sale / download files available for purchase / data CDs” copy. Not selling through the site for now.
  • Maps: known hard problem; not a cutover blocker if no worse than today. Townships maps are cut-outs without a “where is this in Burra?” frame; settlement-period maps are wonderful but Tindale provenance is still untraced.
  • Friday working group: keep meeting Fridays, fix a page at a time on Preview rather than writing giant comment lists. Emlyn to show the change methodology and give permission to edit Preview.

Overall reaction

Reception is strongly positive. Barbara: impressed that “all this information is here”; aesthetic/front-page work and organisational pages matter more than polishing every database. Emlyn: old cPanel site is a liability; get to a class of problems similar to (or better than) live, then kill it.

  • Duplicates (Abbott, Bennett, etc.). Preview shows compiled notes (e.g. Rose Green) that need checking.
