Goal

Every Useful Links listing that currently goes to the Internet Archive also has a copy on this site, and the listing points at both.

Source

  • Follow-on from BHG-004: those rows were Wayback-only. Emlyn asked (11 Sep) to keep a standalone on-site copy as well, with links to both the copy and the snapshot.
  • Useful Links inventory — eight Wayback listings: three West Briton letters, Leadbeater marriages, Fawcett 1838, Ships List, Arman megafauna PDF, Leadbeater land essay.

Scope

In scope

  • One standalone page per Wayback listing under resources/archives/.
  • Useful Links: primary href is our copy; a parenthetical Wayback href stays.
  • Replace the two West Briton “2011 nearest” calendar URLs with real captures.

Out of scope

  • Nested Ships List ship pages (index only; ship names still go to Wayback).
  • Recreating Leadbeater’s marriage search or land-selectors database (Wayback did not keep the JS/data).
  • New directories that were never on Useful Links.
  • Real.

Acceptance criteria

  • All eight Wayback listings on Useful Links have an on-site copy and a Wayback link.
  • West Briton pages keep the Burra/Adelaide letter, not the whole Cornwall issue.
  • Marriages page says the working search is Wayback (JS/data not in the snapshot).
  • Live on Preview. Real not touched.

None. Letter-vs-full-issue and the marriages JS limit are recorded here, not a group vote.
