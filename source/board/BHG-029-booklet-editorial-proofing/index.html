Goal

Take the mechanically converted responsive readers through historical, visual and accessibility proofing before public workshop publication. Conversion is technically validated (BHG-027), but automated text/image parity does not establish editorial accuracy, proper column order, caption pairing or completeness of words embedded inside images.

Sources

  • Full technical report
  • Independent reader QA by title
  • Structured inventory with paths
  • Native PDFs and legacy Publisher HTML preserved on the Dev 8 TB working drive.

Required checks

  1. Visually compare opening, middle and closing pages for each of 38 recovered titles against the native PDF or opened Publisher original; inspect additional map, multi-column, image-heavy, long-table, rotated/imposed or unusual pages.
  2. Triage 122 predominantly image-based pages. Do not automatically OCR every cover: distinguish photographs, maps and art from pages bearing substantial text. For text-bearing images, record a plan for checked transcription or a labelled facsimile. Pay special attention to entire-image booklets #24 The Burra Mines, #29 The Unicorn Brewery, #35 The S. T. Gill Collection.
  3. Resolve Publisher-spread upside-down artwork per individual illustration, never rotate adjacent upright photographs by accident. The current reader has a reader-operated 180° per-figure control. Record confirmed default rotations in explicit source overrides where practical.
  4. Associate captions with the correct image; write meaningful reviewed alt text and credit photographers/illustrators where the source actually does.
  5. Confirm page order within pages on multicolumn or layout-heavy material. Existing PDF and source page IDs are the verification evidence, not a reason to trust heuristic shape position.
  6. Check two empty-export candidates: Burra's Monster Mine p.6, The Kooringa Walk p.9.
  7. Confirm title, contributor and year/edition information, including differences in Burra Fires, Burra Story and Miners' Dugouts. Do not silently merge variant editions.
  8. Review and approve or amend all 39 draft booklet descriptions, clearly distinguishing the absent 28-page The Burra Institute.
  9. Record image/publication permissions and Council/Visitor Centre consultation before enabling public access to original Publisher or PDF files on the publicly addressable Working site.
  10. Rebuild and rerun source-fidelity, asset and mobile/browser checks after changes; retain a per-booklet exception and editorial sign-off record.

Acceptance criteria

  • Every recovered booklet has an explicit editorial decision, with source/PDF reference and reviewed credits.
  • Image-based pages triaged; any necessary transcription or facsimile treatment is complete or clearly restricted from public presentation.
  • Incorrect caption associations, artwork rotations and multicolumn reading order rectified; exceptions no longer silently mislead.
  • Reader links and metadata are approved for workshop release, or individual titles are held back.
  • 39 editorial descriptions reviewed and labelled accordingly.
  • BHG decision about workshop-hosted .pub, PDF and reader availability recorded.
  • BHG-028 release checklist cites reviewed titles and blocks unapproved items.

Boundary

This ticket does not upload any files or modify the archival source. Use the per-title/page override mechanism and recreate responsive pages in the separate working directory. BHG-028 can develop its safe incremental uploader in parallel, but cannot enable the public links before the relevant editorial/permissions decisions.
