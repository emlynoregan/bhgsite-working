Goal

Make the Booklets catalogue, detail pages, responsive readers and original PDF/Publisher downloads reachable on the working site while preserving BHG's staged publication/review arrangement.

Source

  • Working-site implementation plan.
  • 21 September meeting notes: workshop first; Council/Visitor Centre conversation before Preview or Real.
  • 4 September meeting notes: publications/treasurer consultation and booklet IP boundaries.

In scope

  • Verify House Site Library size/upload restrictions and .pub/.pdf headers, including ~141 MB largest Publisher file.
  • Manifest-based, resumable uploads of large non-Git assets, preferably separate from routine page deploys; test no accidental pruning.
  • Deliver the 39-page catalogue/detail set and 38 readers to working-bhg.house-of-ur.com/booklets/.
  • Wire and verify 38 PDF/Publisher binary downloads, including direct URL, content type, downloaded bytes/hash and mobile usability.
  • Implement uploader/staging independently, but obtain BHG-029 per-title editorial/rights decisions and Council/Visitor Centre timing before activating any publicly reachable reader or original-file link.

Out of scope

  • Automatic promotion to Preview, Real, or burrasa.net.
  • Changing historical 2017 catalogue data or putting unrelated National Trust publications online.

Acceptance criteria

  • Working-site Booklets menu/catalogue and 39 pages accessible, with 38 readable HTML editions.
  • All approved download links succeed with correct file name, headers and checksum; no missing assets.
  • Resumable independent uploader and local journal implemented/tested; real House API transfer, quota/max-file check and deploy non-pruning proof are still awaiting authorisation.
  • Publication approvals default to false and are enforced per booklet/format; BHG-029 sign-off and BHG/Council decisions are still outstanding.
  • Working-site smoke tests, navigation and rollback procedure documented.

Stop and ask

Working site is publicly addressable even though it serves as a workshop. Confirm BHG/Bel's intended exposure of .pub and PDF files and the Council/Visitor Centre timing before enabling public downloads. Do not deploy to Preview or Real.

Progress — 19 October 2026

The local technical staging is implemented. Detailed staging report. A source SHA-256 verified manifest covers 800 files / 1,172,003,794 bytes (38 PDFs, 38 Publisher originals, 724 responsive reader files) outside Git, under D:\bhg-booklet-recovery-20261019\booklet-release\. All 38 titles have deterministic target paths under the Working Library only; internal JSON/legacy exports are excluded.

New bhgsite-working/scripts/booklets/assets.py, delivery.py, and test_delivery.py provide a read-only offline plan, explicit per-title/per-format decision gate (all currently unapproved), 1 MiB streamed transfers, full downloaded-byte/hash/type verification, atomic resume journal and exclusive writer lock. A local loopback upload test passed; an intentional real --execute --confirm-publication attempt correctly refused before API write/auth, because publication is not authorised. Read-only House API listing confirmed the BHG Working Site mapping.

Not yet Done. No files or reader links have been released; the normal page-publisher fail-closed hold remains. Actual House upload limit and ~141 MB Publisher support, real HTTP content-disposition, idempotence across regular deploys, CDN readback, BHG-029 editorial sign-off and final staged publication are unverified or awaiting approval. Do not claim the booklet collection is publicly readable yet.
