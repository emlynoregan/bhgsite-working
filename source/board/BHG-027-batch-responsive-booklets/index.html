Goal

Apply the validated reader converter to all 38 recovered booklets, giving the new Booklets detail pages a complete responsive reading edition, with image assets and page anchors.

Source

  • Reader prototype ticket.
  • Working-site plan and structured catalogue.

In scope

  • Batch convert all 626 source HTML pages, keeping unique source mapping.
  • Handle multi-column layouts, pictures, captions, maps, references and pagination with documented per-booklet correction files.
  • Reconcile different editions and catalogued/actual page counts without mislabelling.
  • Automated text/image/link validation and mobile/desktop sample visual inspections for each booklet.
  • Update the catalogue with reader status and work requiring human editorial review.

Out of scope

  • Uploading binary source/PDF assets.
  • Publishing to Preview or Real, or inventing missing The Burra Institute content.

Acceptance criteria

  • 38 responsive reader editions built, usable on desktop and mobile.
  • 626 source pages accounted for; reading sequence and page anchors verified.
  • No missing images/links or silently dropped text, and correct source/credit footers.
  • Exception report and source-vs-output audits, with unresolved items marked for review.
  • All original Publisher and PDF copies remain unchanged.

Stop and ask

Review the converter against the pilot and design first. If exceptions dominate, revise the design rather than ship hundreds of brittle one-off pages.
