Goal

The mine timeline on Preview again carries the full [more] expansions from the live cPanel site, starting with Thomas Pickett’s death. Cutover is parity: we should not go backwards from burrasa.net.

Source

  • Found 12 September 2026 while checking the Johnny Green “shepherd who found the ore” story against Preview. The 1851 entry still links [more] to #pickett, but the note is a one-liner. Legacy “Death of a Shepherd” is gone.
  • Preview mine timeline — “Thomas Pickett — shepherd credited with discovering the Burra Burra Mine; later worked as a timber carter for the smelter.”
  • Legacy mine timeline — two paragraphs: paid £10 then another £10; died a pauper in November 1851 after falling into a fire at a deserted shepherd’s hut on Diprose Creek; unmarked grave in the old Kooringa Cemetery; “Remember Pickett.”
  • The truncation is in the first static rebuild (bhgsite2026 83b606a), not a later edit. Same Notes block also collapsed tributors, and the Gill 1850 caption still has a literal horse whims[more].
  • Cutover bar from 28 August / working-site news: parity with burrasa.net, not a rewrite.

Scope

In scope

  • Restore the Pickett expansion on /mine/timeline/ from the legacy “Death of a Shepherd” text (keep #pickett so the 1851 [more] still works).
  • Compare that page’s other [more] notes with legacy (timber, tributors / tut, first smelter, Gulf Road, shears, horse whims, launder, poppet-head, kibble, and any others still on burrasa.net) and restore what the rebuild dropped.
  • Fix leftover literal [more] in captions (Gill 1850 at least).
  • Live on Preview. Real not touched.

Out of scope

  • Rewriting Pickett from the 1851 inquest or Auhl (that can follow; this ticket is restore-what-we-had).
  • Identifying Johnny Green with Pickett (johnny-green.md).
  • Town / buildings timelines unless the same missing-note pattern is obvious while doing the mine page.

Acceptance criteria

  • Preview #pickett has the legacy death account (pauper; fire at a deserted hut; unmarked grave; “Remember Pickett”), not only the timber-carter line.
  • Other mine-timeline [more] expansions that exist on burrasa.net are present on Preview, or the ticket notes a deliberate skip.
  • No literal [more] left in the Gill caption.
  • Live on Preview. Real not touched.

Stop and ask

None for restoring the legacy wording. If the 1851 inquest (Hallett’s Springs; body found by a boy; SAMA paid funeral expenses) should replace the Diprose Creek hut story rather than sit beside it, stop and ask Barbara before rewriting.
